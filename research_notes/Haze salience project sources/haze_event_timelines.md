## 2026 season: draft claims verified or corrected

### Takeaway
A major 2026 haze season is real and reported by many outlets. Draft claim 2 (5 Oct Greenpeace) and the 16 Oct stock fall are broadly supported, but there are corrections. ## 2026: Singapore PSI definition and data source

### Takeaway
NEA reports 24-hr PSI by region, hourly, and publishes historical 24-hr PSI on data.gov.sg. Numeric bands were not retrieved from a search result; the standard NEA bands are stated from background knowledge only and must be checked.

### Cited Findings
- PSI computed from six pollutants, 24-hr rolling, hourly updates by 5 regions — [data.gov.sg dataset page](https://data.gov.sg/dataset/historical-24-hr-psi); regional CSV Apr 2014 onward (listings end Dec 2024 / Jul 2026; check currency) — [data.gov.sg](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_b4cf557f8750260d229c49fd768e11ed/view)
- Media streak counting: a day counts unhealthy if PSI exceeds 100 at any point in any part of Singapore — [Star summary](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/10/16/singapore-is-experiencing-12th-consecutive-day-of-unhealthy-air-the-longest-since-2015-haze-crisis); this differs from a strict 24-hr PSI >100 definition.
- THPA haze pollution trigger: PSI in unhealthy range in any part of Singapore for 24 hours or more — [Star 19 Oct](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/10/19/palm-oil-stocks-tumble-as-spore-probes-haze-links)
- Background (NOT sourced this session): Good 0-50, Moderate 51-100, Unhealthy 101-200, Very Unhealthy 201-300, Hazardous >300.

### Inferences
- To cross-check streak dates, recompute from the data.gov.sg regional 24-hr PSI series and state which rule (max across regions, any hour) is used.
